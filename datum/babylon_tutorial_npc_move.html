babylon_tutorial_npc_move
/datum/babylon_tutorial_npc_move

One NPC walking to a tile for a Move NPC action: a step every move delay along a JPS path (get_path_to, the game's pathfinder, the same one AI mobs use), found again when the way is blocked. Crawls when the NPC can't stand (a lying mob moves, only slower). Ends on arrival (the action's On arrival actions run), or gives up at the deadline (a note for the builder).

Vars

doors"id", "public" or "all": which doors it goes through (the action's Doors setting).
pathThe tiles still to walk (the next one first).
stepsHow many steps it walked; why it waits right now (for the give-up note).
trigger_refThe trigger whose actions started the walk ("until the next step" of the follow-ups).

Procs

arrivedThe NPC is there: on the tile, or next to it when something solid stands on it.
walk_stepOne step: arrive, give up, wait, or move to the next tile of the path. Tests call it directly. It may sleep while the pathfinder works (get_path_to); a timer calls it.
