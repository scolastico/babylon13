Vars

creativeEvery player gets the Creative panel.
kind"world" (an ordinary world), "tutorial" (a throwaway play instance) or "tutorial_edit" (an admin editing a pending tutorial).

Procs

can_save_back_tutorialA save goes back to the pending tutorial from here (G1b): an admin's tutorial edit world.
can_submit_tutorialA tutorial can be submitted from here (G1b): a tutorial-capable ordinary world.
creative_enabledEvery player gets the Creative panel.
creative_machines_activeThe creative machines' infinite behaviour runs (creative/machines.dm): Creative worlds, tutorial instances (an authored scenario keeps its creative power and air, though its players have no Creative panel) and Creative Plane maps (the map's own power and air, whatever the Creative flag).
delam_containedA supermatter delamination only explodes: no singularity, tesla ball or resonance cascade (creative/supermatter.dm). Wherever creative machines run, so a Creative Plane map always contains it, with or without the Creative flag.
is_creative_planeCreative Plane worlds (catalog group "creative_plane": ids creative_plane_s/m/l).
plays_tutorial_on_joinA player who joins is put into the tutorial's flow (station_round.dm): play instances. Admins editing a tutorial test-run it themselves.
round_starts_at_onceThe round starts by itself as soon as the world is up, with no owner hold (station_round.dm): tutorial instances.
savesCheckpoints are written (autosaves, the owner's save, a save on stop): persistent worlds that are not tutorial instances. A forced save (a tutorial submission) ignores it.
saves_charactersA checkpoint keeps the players' characters (bodies, inventories) and per-player records (station_characters.dm, state.dm): every world but tutorial instances. A forced tutorial snapshot never keeps them either (babylon_world_saves_characters(for_tutorial)).
startup_decay_enabledFresh Creative Planes preserve their authored floors and continuous perimeter catwalk. Ordinary maps keep Skyrat's configured startup decay, including in Creative worlds.
stop_savesA managed stop saves first: exactly the worlds that save.
storyteller_inertOnly the inert Babylon storyteller runs, with no random events (storyteller.dm): the Babylon build, tutorial instances and Creative Plane maps.
tutorial_capableTutorials can be played and edited here: the station build with Creative enabled.
unsaved_as_tutorialThe stop message says the world is a tutorial, which is never saved (admission.dm).
