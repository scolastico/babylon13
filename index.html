  • modular_babylon
    • Babylon13 cluster: lobby, supervisor and worlds - Babylon13 runs many small game worlds behind one lobby. Players pick or create a world in the lobby; the supervisor starts it as its own DreamDaemon process, admits the player with a one-time ticket, saves the world while it runs and stops it again when it is empty. This document describes what players, owners and administrators can do, how worlds are saved, and how to run the cluster. The private Babylon prototype is separate and is not migrated or restarted. VALIDATION.md records what was tested and how; UPSTREAM_HOOKS.md lists the upstream files Babylon changes.
    • Upstream integration hooks - Babylon is opt-in: compile babylon.dme (the drone builder) and babylon-station.dme (normal tg rounds on the map catalog's station maps). The upstream tgstation.dme is unchanged. The fifteen modified upstream DM files use hooks guarded by BABYLON_BUILDER and marked BABYLON13. Ordinary SPLURT builds compile those blocks out.
    • Groundwork validation - Validated locally on 2026-11-09, based on upstream master 58cd83015ba7a528c87ad4c894785caa6e655265.
