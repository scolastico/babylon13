Procs

attack_self_secondaryRight-click in hand: the next mode.
choose_objectThe tutorial object a click means: the spawner of a clicked NPC, else one on the tile (a list to choose from when there are several).
move_objectMoves the armed object to a tile. Its settings and the links to it stay; an NPC spawner takes its NPC along. Zones and picked tiles are settings: they stay where they are.
moving_objectThe object the wand is armed to move, if any.
picking_objectThe object whose zone is being picked, if any.
start_pickingStarts picking a zone for an object's setting: the next two clicks are its corners. A "tile" setting takes one click.
sync_viewShows the images to the holder while the wand is in a hand in a creative world, else hides them. Rebuilds them when tutorial objects changed. force: rebuild anyway (new focus).
toggle_movingArms the wand: its next click on a tile moves the object there. Arming it again for the same object cancels.
usable_byThe wand works for this mob: creative world, its own living body, holding the wand.
use_onA click on something with the wand, in the current mode.
