modular_babylon/code/tutorial/actions.dm

/proc/babylon_tutorial_fill_text"[player]" in builder text becomes the player's name.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_guide_messageThe friendly "Guide" chat box, headed with the speaker's name. Lines are kept.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_until_fields"Show until" for actions that show something: the next step, some seconds, or a trigger.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_speakA speaker says something to every player of the run. TRUE when at least one heard it spoken.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_speak_toA speaker says something to one player only, in say style with a chat bubble over the speaker. When the player could not hear it (too far, another level, the speaker is out cold or dead), it arrives as a Guide box headed with the speaker's name instead.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_speakerThe atom that speaks for an id: a spawner's NPC, a marker's object, or the marker itself (named after its speaker name) when its tile is empty.
BABYLON_TUTORIAL_POINT_FLIGHTA speaker points at a target for the run's players: an arrow flies from the speaker to the target (an image only they see) and players nearby read " points at .". The flying arrow fades out as it arrives: the Point action's own arrow already bounces over the target, and a second one resting next to it was the two-arrows bug (spec 12 #14). How long a speaker's arrow flies to its target (deciseconds, as upstream pointing), and how long it then takes to fade out over the target's own arrow.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_image_keyThe key of a run image (show_image()): what it shows, and on which atom.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_dress_outlineMakes an image an outlined copy of an atom's current look, attached to it.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_soundsSound name => file: the sounds a tutorial may play.
/datum/babylon_tutorial_completeThe "Tutorial complete!" screen (tgui BabylonTutorialComplete).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_body_target_fields"Apply to": every player or an NPC spawner's NPC.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_harm_effectsEffect key => label: what a Damage / effect action can do. Curated for teaching: the four damage types (medicine), wounds and a bleeding cut (gauze, splints), fire (stop, drop and roll), a knockdown, blurred or lost sight (eye care) and a few curable illnesses. Nothing that takes the player's control away for long (sleep, unconsciousness) or destroys a body.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_harmApplies one Damage / effect action's effect to a body. God mode (an unhurtable NPC, a builder in god mode) protects. Returns TRUE when it applied.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_healHeals a living body: fully, or one damage type by an amount.
