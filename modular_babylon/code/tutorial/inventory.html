modular_babylon/code/tutorial/inventory.dm

BABYLON_TUTORIAL_LOADOUT_MAXEntries one loadout holds at most, and items inside them (all levels together).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_slotsSlot flag => label, in the order a loadout dresses a body (uniform first: pockets, belt and ID hang on it).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_placeWhere an entry goes, as the window names it: "slot:", "hand:1", "hand:2" or "packed".
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_place_optionsEvery place an item can be added to, for the window: list(list(value, label)).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_inventory_keptItems an inventory change never takes away: the builder's tools (babylon_builder_tool). They stay where they are; one inside something that goes is taken out first (to a free hand, else the floor). A loadout item meant for their hand goes to the other hand or the floor.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_kept_insideBuilder tools inside something (a wand in a backpack), at any depth.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_player_holdersWhat must come out of thing (an item) before it is deleted because it holds a player: a pAI card with its pAI, an MMI or a brain with its brainmob, a held mob. Mobs are never deleted with what holds them (babylon_tutorial_removable() has the same rule for Remove objects). From a bag (an item with storage) only the part that holds the player comes out, whole; any other item that holds one (the card, the MMI itself) is kept whole: the list then holds thing itself.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_rescue_playersTakes what holds a player out of item (babylon_tutorial_player_holders()) onto the floor at tile. Returns TRUE when item itself holds one and so must not be deleted.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_json_safeA value JSON keeps as it is (null, a number, a text, or a list of those): settings that hold anything else (a path, a reference) are left out of a loadout.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_capture_loadoutA body's loadout: what it wears and holds, with settings and contents. Returns list("loadout" = entries, "left_out" = names of items that can't be in a tutorial).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_trim_contentsCuts an entry's contents down to the budget left (budget[1], items inside bags). Returns how many items it cut.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_entryOne item as a loadout entry (its place is added by the caller).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_clean_loadoutlist("value" = cleaned loadout) or list("error" = why it was refused).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_clean_loadout_itemOne item of a loadout (depth 0) or of its contents. budget[1] counts the items inside.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_insideItems inside an entry's containers, all levels.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_loadout_viewA loadout as the window shows it: list(list(place, where, name, path, inside)).
/proc/babylon_tutorial_set_inventoryChanges a body's inventory. mode: "clear_all" (everything worn and held), "clear_hands" (held items) or "loadout" (everything, then the loadout). drop_old: the removed items fall to the floor instead of being deleted. Kept items (babylon_tutorial_inventory_kept) stay. Returns the items the loadout created.
/proc/babylon_tutorial_make_loadout_itemMakes one loadout item on a tile, with its name, settings, reagents and contents.
