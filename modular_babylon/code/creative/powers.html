BABYLON_CREATIVE_UNSTUCK_RANGEHow far (tiles) noclip-off and teleports look for a free spot.
/proc/babylon_creative_set_xrayX-ray: see turfs, objects and mobs through walls. Every living body type recomputes its sight its own way (carbon from the trait, silicons from their modules, simple animals from initial()), and only some read TRAIT_XRAY_VISION, so the power is applied by a hook on the sight update that every type ends in (COMSIG_MOB_UPDATE_SIGHT, see babylon_creative_apply_sight); the trait stays as the creative trait source (carbon reads it too, harmlessly).
/proc/babylon_creative_set_fullbrightFullbright: the lighting plane's cutoff is raised to fully bright (same hook as X-ray). The cutoff from before is put back on off, for the body types that never recompute it.
/proc/babylon_creative_sync_sight_hookHooks the body's sight update while X-ray or fullbright is on, unhooks it when both are off, and applies the change at once.
/proc/babylon_creative_turf_blockedTRUE when a body cannot stand on this turf: walls, dense machines, anything blocking.
/proc/babylon_creative_free_turfThe nearest free turf to center (itself included) on its z-level, preferring floors over space, or null when there is none within BABYLON_CREATIVE_UNSTUCK_RANGE.
/proc/babylon_creative_unstuckMoves a body standing inside a wall (noclip just turned off) to the nearest free spot.
/proc/babylon_creative_healFull heal; revives a dead body.
/proc/babylon_creative_without_godmodeRuns one synchronous authoring operation, retaining every original source of immunity.
/proc/babylon_creative_revoke_powersTakes back every creative power from every body (Creative switched off at runtime).
/proc/babylon_creative_belt_toolsThe tools the kit's toolbelt holds.
/proc/babylon_creative_kit_typesEverything in the kit, as the Kits tab lists it.
/proc/babylon_creative_give_toolsThe basic tool kit: a toolbelt with the everyday tools, plus an RCD (with matter), an RPD and insulated gloves. Human bodies wear the belt and gloves when those slots are free; the rest goes to the hands, else to the floor. Returns the created items.
/proc/babylon_creative_give_wandThe tutorial wand (tutorial-capable worlds), in hand. Null while it does not exist. world_edit: the world-edit wand instead (the area tools, same rules).
