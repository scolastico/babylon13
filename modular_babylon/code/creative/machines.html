BABYLON_CREATIVE_MACHINE_FIELDSPersisted settings of the creative machines, added to the save's extra_fields.
/proc/babylon_creative_machine_typesThe spawnable creative machine types (the Creative panel offers these). Each base type is a source; the named subtypes are presets of the same machine.
/proc/babylon_creative_examineShared examine lines: what the machine does, how to change it, or that it is inert here.
/proc/babylon_creative_contextScreentip for the settings gesture, shown where settings may change.
/proc/babylon_creative_can_configureSettings may only change in a Creative world, by someone who can still reach the machine.
/proc/babylon_creative_promptOne settings prompt: kind "list", "number" or "text". Tests answer from GLOB.babylon_creative_test_answers.
/proc/babylon_creative_ask_modeAsks for one of the modes (display text = mode); returns the mode or null when cancelled.
/proc/babylon_creative_gas_choicesGases a creative canister can produce: every gas in the standard gas list.
/proc/babylon_creative_reagent_allowedReagents a creative tank can produce: those chemistry may synthesize (REAGENT_CAN_BE_SYNTHESIZED, the chem dispenser/synthesizer rule, which keeps out admin-only and special reagents) and none of the ERP reagents (sfw.dm).
/proc/babylon_creative_automated_extraWhat would harm the round when a creative disposal source made it every few seconds, on top of the panel's blocklist: grenades, bomb cores, tank transfer valves and brain holders.
/proc/babylon_creative_item_blockedItem types a creative disposal source never produces: everything the Creative panel may not spawn (babylon_creative_spawn_blocked, spawning.dm: the one spawn blocklist, ERP items included), plus babylon_creative_automated_extra().
/proc/babylon_creative_inner_item_blockedThings a produced item may not carry inside it: only the harmful half of the blocklist (babylon_creative_type_harmful) plus babylon_creative_automated_extra(). The entry rules (abstract, ABSTRACT, no icon) do not apply to contents: a revolver's internal cylinder and an implanter's implant are ABSTRACT, and the item breaks without them.
/proc/babylon_creative_item_allowedItems a creative disposal source may produce: /obj/item types only (the panel's blocklist already refuses abstract types and ABSTRACT items), nothing babylon_creative_item_blocked().
/proc/babylon_creative_item_matches"[name] ([path])" entries of the allowed items whose name or path contains the search, sorted, at most limit of them.
